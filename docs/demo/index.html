Built for Indian schools

The rules your school already follows, handled for you

Government returns, consent law, SMS regulations and the way Indian parents pay are part of the product, not add-ons.

DPDP Act 2023

Consent and accountability

Guardian consent is recorded at admission. Aadhaar is encrypted with only the last four digits shown. An audit log records every change, sign-in and export, and cannot be edited.

UDISE+ · APAAR · RTE

Government reports in two clicks

Class strength by gender and social category, PEN and APAAR IDs with consent, the RTE 25% quota list, and attendance eligibility below 75%. Everything exports to Excel.

TRAI DLT

SMS and WhatsApp that comply

DLT-registered templates and sender IDs, absence alerts and fee reminders with each family's exact due amount. Messages go through the school's own gateway.

UPI · Razorpay

Fees the way parents pay

UPI, cards and net banking from the parent's phone, plus counter collection with UTR and cheque details. Sibling fees are collected in one go, and every rupee posts to accounts automatically.

QR verification

Certificates nobody can fake

Transfer, bonafide, character and experience certificates carry a serial number and a QR code. Anyone can scan it to confirm the certificate is genuine or has been revoked.

12 languages

Speaks your staff's language

English, हिन्दी, বাংলা, मराठी, తెలుగు, தமிழ், ગુજરાતી, ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, ଓଡ଼ିଆ and অসমীয়া. The school picks which ones to offer. It works on a ₹8,000 phone and a principal's laptop alike.

Live GPS

Parents know where the bus is

The driver taps Start trip, or a standard GPS tracker reports in. Parents see the bus and its ETA, and get a “bus approaching” alert. No hardware purchase is required.

Phone app · push

An app on every parent's phone

Install from the browser on Android or iPhone, with alerts for absence, fees, results and the bus. A store-ready wrapper is included for Play Store and App Store listings.

One login, eleven views

Permissions are set per role and per module (view, add, edit, delete), and checked by the server on every request. Teachers see only their sections, and parents see only their own children.

Where we lead
  • India compliance built in: DPDP consent and an immutable audit log, DLT templates, UDISE+, APAAR and RTE reports.
  • Live bus tracking that works with a driver's phone or any standard GPS tracker, with “bus approaching” alerts to parents.
  • Hostel and Tally export in the same product, with no add-ons to buy.
  • Published, per-school pricing with a free trial. No sales call needed to know the cost.
  • Granular role permissions (view, add, edit, delete per module) with row-level scope, verified by 845 automated checks.
  • QR-verifiable certificates, admission CRM with a public form, and a POS store in the same product.
Where we trail today
  • The phone app is an installable web app with push alerts. Play Store and App Store listings need a store build from the included wrapper.
  • 12 Indian languages cover menus and key screens; Fedena lists 20+ and Teachmint 90+ on its classroom panel.
  • No biometric attendance devices or classroom hardware (Fedena and Teachmint list these); attendance uses ID-card scanning.
  • A newer brand, without the large installed bases competitors report.
